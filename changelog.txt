Xincraft Changelog
==================

1.19.0
 - Carriage: a rideable passenger minecart in maroon lacquer, trimmed in bronze; an inspector rail
   reads 1 for an empty one and 2 for one with a passenger
 - Linking Bar: hitches a carriage, jingle cart or another locomotive behind a locomotive, which
   tows it; the carts are joined by a pivoting bronze drawbar so they take corners and slopes
   without jamming
 - Locomotive Cart handbrake: hold jump while riding to brake to a stop
 - Buoys: two-block marker buoys for still water, linked into courses up to 32 blocks a leg with
   the linking bar; right-click one to switch its lamp off, and boats on the course stop and
   wait at it, burning no fuel, until it's switched back on
 - RC Upgrade: a motorboat with it follows buoy courses on its own, slowing for sharp turns and
   stopping if it runs aground; rose-tinted glasses show the courses and start and stop RC boats

1.18.0
 - Party Boat: a bronze-lined motorboat with a jukebox; plays its disc while on the move, and
   takes the sculk sensor upgrade
 - Rose-Tinted Glasses: worn or held, read the essence in elemental flora, one at a time or all
   of those around an Apothecary Altar

1.17.0
 - Apothecary Altar: burns plants and furnace fuel into elemental essence for the flora within
   4 blocks; which plant gives which element is set by item tags
 - Elemental flora now deepen in colour as they fill, from washed-out to their full colour

1.16.0
 - Nature magic: a mortar and pestle grinds vines and flowers into seven elemental powders
   (Terra, Aqua, Ignis, Aer, Vita, Lux, Umbra)
 - Elemental flora: an element's powder on any small flower turns it into a glowing store of
   that element's essence, for rituals to come; broken, it's lost

1.15.0
 - Shimmer Upgrade (formerly Refractive): riders of an ender minecart, locomotive cart or
   motorboat are invisible, but for the odd pink glint
 - Shimmer Lily: exotic pollen on a wither rose; Invisibility for players nearby

1.14.0
 - Motorboats: any boat + a propeller (tin and bronze); auto-cruise under fuel power, one for
   every wood plus the bamboo motor raft
 - Upgrades now fit motorboats: shrieker, energy, plus the new ice breaker and speed upgrades

1.13.0
 - Mixing tray reworked: four different flowers make a mash, honeycomb turns it into pollen
 - Moonlit Dandelion: exotic pollen on a golden dandelion; Night Vision for players nearby

1.12.0
 - Exotic flowers: mixing tray, exotic pollen, Mending Lilac (regeneration) and Firefly Petunia
   (soft light)
 - Glowing leaves: exotic pollen on any leaves makes them glow faintly

1.11.0
 - Heat turbine (tin) and steam turbine (bronze, uses water): burn fuel into Forge Energy
 - A full turbine stops lighting new fuel

1.10.0
 - Charging rail (bronze): stores Forge Energy and charges locomotives on it
 - Energy upgrade: fuel charges the locomotive's battery, and the battery drives it

1.9.0
 - Jingle cart: plays music discs while rolling; loaded and unloaded by hoppers
 - Cart upgrades: shrieker, sculk sensor and energy; removed with shears
 - Recipes unlock in the recipe book
 - Version set in one place (gradle.properties)

Earlier
 - Bronze, bronze armor (step-up set bonus) and bronze block
 - Tin armor trims, tin trim material and the reinforced armor trim
 - Tin spyglass (night vision)
 - Tin, padded, honey, bouncy, inspector and ender rails; ender minecart, locomotive cart
